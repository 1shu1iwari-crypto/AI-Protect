Jump to the lab

Scams are workflows, not isolated messages.

Each step looks harmless on its own. The danger lies in the progression, and the moment it culminates in an irreversible transfer.

  1. 1

    The hook

    A "refund" or "KYC update" arrives with a link from a sender masquerading as an official service.

    Looks normal
  2. 2

    The pressure

    A caller claims authority. Urgency and secrecy build: don't tell anyone, stay on the line.

    Pressure rising
  3. 3

    The transfer

    An outgoing QR or UPI intent routes money to an unfamiliar payee.

    Money moves

Read the order. Warn at the action.

One local engine tracks evidence across channels and intervenes only when accumulated evidence meets a risky payment action.

  1. Step 1

    Explicit user choice

    Paste, share, scan a QR, or flag a call. Nothing is analysed until you explicitly request an inspection.

  2. Step 2

    Coarse local extraction

    Detect authority claims, urgency cues, OTP requests, APK links, and payee changes as behavioral tactics rather than raw text.

  3. Step 3

    Temporal workflow tracking

    Track the multi-step trajectory: Pretext → Pressure → Credential solicitation → Outgoing transfer intent.

  4. Step 4

    Action-gated pause

    Deliver a clear, explained warning with an independent verification path. Repeated alerts are suppressed to prevent fatigue.

Guarded hybrid decision policy: Deterministic workflow rules and temporal transitions decide. Lightweight local ML corroborates by at most three score points and can never trigger an alert independently.

Validation and benchmark evidence

Results generated directly by npm run evaluate across all disclosed synthetic workflows.

Validation scope and boundaries. These tests measure performance on curated synthetic workflows rather than live carrier audio or production banking telemetry. Holdouts evaluate compositions of known tactics. Field trials with regional languages, device battery profiling, and vulnerable-user accessibility studies remain future work.

Signals first, then human analyst judgement.

With explicit user consent, devices share coarse tactic fingerprints without raw text, account numbers, or values. Repeated similar fingerprints form unverified candidates for analyst review. Nothing is blocked automatically.

Analyst review gate

Analyst review updates the candidate status locally. It does not publish threat intelligence or block payees.

Stored only in this memory field. Requires SCAMGUARD_REVIEW_TOKEN on the server.

Android Companion

The same engine, packaged for mobile.

The companion app bundles this exact detector in a hardened WebView with zero network permissions. Two distinct build flavours keep permissions minimal and honest:

  • Play flavour: manual signals and shared text or QR codes, with zero microphone or accessibility service access.
  • Hackathon flavour: optional consented speech recognition running strictly on-device, with zero cloud transmission.
Privacy by Construction

Less data, on purpose.

Architecture decisions designed to prevent surveillance and protect confidential financial interactions:

  • Raw text and voice audio are never stored; only derived, coarse tactic labels survive.
  • Payee handles and exact currency amounts are discarded immediately after heuristic validation.
  • Suspicious links are analyzed strictly as text; destinations are never fetched or visited.
  • Pattern sharing and telemetry are strictly decoupled, opt-in actions.

Transparent product limits

A working reference implementation for scam workflow detection, not a commercial banking product.

What it is

A web and Android reference implementation of workflow-level scam detection with explainable, privacy-preserving evidence trails.

What it is not

It cannot intercept ordinary cellular-call audio or monitor other third-party banking apps, and it never moves money. Transfers here are purely simulated.

Roadmap

Real consented community datasets, device battery benchmarks, native OS share targets, partner payment gateway webhooks, and independent security audits.
