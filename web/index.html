EVIDENCE, NOT A CLAIM

Put the pause to the test.

These results are measured on 52 AI-authored synthetic workflows, including a separate challenge suite with two known missed patterns. They do not establish performance on real scams or real phones.

What still needs validation

Consented or licensed real conversations, regional-language coverage, paraphrase attacks, device battery and latency, campaign poisoning, and usability with vulnerable users. Holdout scenarios test composition of known tactics; they are not proof of unseen-scam generalization.
